Meet your hosts

Hosted by Evan & Amy H.

Cave Hill Farm has been in Evan's family for six generations. With Rockingham Retreats, he and Amy have opened a few quiet corners of it to guests. You can hike with your dog, sit by the fire under the trees, or head into the Valley for local restaurants, ice cream and Cave Hill Farms Brewery. As they put it, this is the Shenandoah Valley "the way we've always loved it: quiet, beautiful, and best enjoyed with good company."
